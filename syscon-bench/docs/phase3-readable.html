Brief read

  • Elasticsearch — fastest overall query average on 5M (steady 12.8 ms). Filters cheap. Full-text / aggregation p99 rise under spikes. Disk ~1.54 GB.
  • ClickHouse — higher exact-match cost; strong on sum(length(message)). Largest measured disk among 5M engines (~2.0 GB). async_insert did not shrink disk.
  • Loki — small at 1M (~0.47 GB) but ~2.34 GB if scaled ×5; highest CPU/mem (~9 GB). Stream+chunk+index layout costs more disk than VL.
  • VictoriaLogs — smallest disk even @5M scale (~1.15 GB). ES-like filter latency; full-text/agg p99 similar to ES under spikes.
